I am Ziqi (Adam) Chen, a Computer Science & Engineering undergraduate at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (2023–2027, expected). My work centers on large language models and LLM-based multi-agent systems.

I am currently a research intern in the UChicago Summer Research Fellowship program, and previously a research assistant at the CUHK-Shenzhen FreedomAI Lab and the Shenzhen Research Institute of Big Data. On the industry side I have worked as a founding engineer at Mortar and an NLP engineer at Lalamove.

My research focuses on making LLM systems more structure-aware and better coordinated: representing documents as hierarchical trees for accurate retrieval and analysis, and learning task-adaptive communication topologies for multi-agent systems.

selected publications

  1. DocMaster: A Hierarchical Structure-Aware System for Document Analysis
    Ziqi Chen, Yingli Zhou, Fangyuan Zhang, and 3 more authors
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2607.08539, Jul 2026
    Abs arXiv Bib HTML

    Large language models struggle to analyze complex documents such as academic papers and financial reports because existing approaches flatten documents into plain text, discarding structural information. DocMaster parses documents into hierarchical document trees that preserve original layouts and constructs a structure-aware semantic index enabling accurate document filtering and in-depth analysis, with an interactive interface for uploading document collections, building semantic indices, filtering via natural-language queries, and question answering over filtered results.
