## [DocMaster: A Hierarchical Structure-Aware System for Document Analysis](https://doc-master.github.io/)

DocMaster parses documents into hierarchical document trees preserving original layouts and constructs a structure-aware semantic index that enables accurate document filtering and in-depth analysis. See the [paper website](https://doc-master.github.io/) for more.

## [MAS-Designer: Learning Task-Adaptive Communication Topologies for LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems](/assets/pdf/MASDesigner.pdf)

MAS-Designer formulates multi-agent communication topology optimization as a graph pruning problem and solved it via Group Relative Policy Optimization with multi-sample averaged rewards.
