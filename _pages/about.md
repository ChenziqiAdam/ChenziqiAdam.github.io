I am **Ziqi (Adam) Chen**, a senior undergraduate student in the School of Data Science at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, pursuing a bachelor degree in Computer Science and Engineering.

I am fortunate to conduct research with [Prof. Hongyuan Zha](https://sds.cuhk.edu.cn/en/teacher/65), [Prof. Xiaopeng Li](https://williampixell.github.io/), and [Prof. Yixiang Fang](https://fangyixiang.github.io/) at CUHK-Shenzhen. Currently, I am doing research with [Prof. Kexin Pei](https://sites.google.com/site/kexinpeisite/) and [Prof. Tian Li](https://litian96.github.io/) at UChicago.

My research interests center on **adaptive language models**. I am particularly interested in enabling LLMs to adapt their behavior to the capabilities of
their collaborators and the demands of different tasks, with topics including multi-agent collaboration, reinforcement learning and AI for Science. I am also interested in applying these techniques to AI for Science, especially scientific reasoning and software.

I am actively seeking PhD opportunities starting from 2027 Fall.

See my [publications](/publications/) and [CV](/assets/pdf/CV.pdf) for more.
